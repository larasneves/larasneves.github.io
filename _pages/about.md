**Ph.D. Student** @CMU advised by Prof. [Eric P. Xing](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~epxing/) and @IST, advised by  Prof. [André Martins](https://andre-martins.github.io/)

I am a 1st-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science, jointly at Carnegie Mellon University and Instituto Superior Técnico. My research focuses on **world and agent models**, investigating how to improve reasoning, planning, prediction, and adaptation in foundational models interacting with dynamic environments.

I completed my MSc in Data Science and Engineering, building on a background in Biomedical Engineering. During my Master's, I developed novel algorithms for online multi-label learning with concept drift, contributing to adaptive, scalable learning systems.
